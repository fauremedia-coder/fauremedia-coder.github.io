# Product: Romain Faure portfolio (recruiter version)

## Who it is for
Recruiters and hiring managers for communication and marketing internships (agencies, brand, PR, content), from February 2027. They usually arrive from a link in a CV or cover letter, on a laptop, and give it one or two minutes.

A second version for people booking photo and video shoots is planned later. Keep the two separate.

## What the page must prove
- Romain is a communication student who also makes the work: art direction, reels for clients, live footage, photography.
- He can take a brief (a show, a brand, a magazine) and turn it into images that do a job.
- He is available from February 2027, and it is easy to reach him.

## Principles
- The work comes first; the name and the interface stay small.
- Limited capacity (LC4MP): captions under images instead of over them, no filters or menus to decide on, and longer text available on request (hover or "Read the process").
- Every claim comes from Romain's CV, his own words or the work itself. No invented numbers, clients or quotes.

- Moved away from: numbered sections, pill buttons, cards, tag chips, light grey background (v1-contact-sheet).
