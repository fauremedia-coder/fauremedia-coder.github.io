Poster shoots for The JR Scheme

Photographer and director · Spring 2026 to now

The JR Scheme is a stand-up comedy night run by Jacob Stewart and Robert Kim. I shoot the photos for their posters. Each photo sits next to the poster it became, with graphics by the show's designer.

The JR Scheme is a stand-up comedy night run by Jacob Stewart and Robert Kim. I shoot the photos for their posters. Each photo sits next to the poster it became, with graphics by the show's designer.

3 July 2026 show

A recurring theme in the posters is Jacob and Robert fighting each other. Here it takes an unexpected turn: Robert turns out to be far more dangerous than Jacob thought.

When I saw this hallway with its red carpet and marble walls, it felt very Vatican-coded, so we started from the idea that Robert was possessed.

5 June 2026 show

That day we had access to the Council Chamber, a historic meeting room inside the Amrâth hotel. The set was beautiful, but we couldn't move anything, so I had to find an interesting frame for the action to happen in.

I placed them under this lamp by the chimney so those elements would pair with the graphic design and bring a sense of symmetry to it.

1 May 2026 show

Same idea here: using the elements around us to bring symmetry to the poster. I wanted Jacob doing dangerous things in the skate park and had him try a few ideas.

The vision was a politician trying to look cool skateboarding with the kids.

7 August 2026 show

These photos were taken the same day as the one for 3 July. This one is more lighthearted, and they used two of the images to make the post more eye-catching.

Its strong suit is definitely how well the colors of the set match the graphic design.

Live photography

Concerts · Ongoing

Shooting in low light is often challenging, but I use these constraints to capture the energy of the performer, as if they were in a zone of their own.

I choose my angles in the moment, depending on who is in front of me and how I perceive them. It's later, during editing, that I can twist the light and colors and reveal what I pictured in my head the moment I took the shot.
